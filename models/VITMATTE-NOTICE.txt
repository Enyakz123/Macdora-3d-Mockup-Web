Input: RGB /255, then mean/std .5; concatenate trimap /255 as fourth channel.
Pad bottom/right with zeros to a multiple of 32. Output is alpha, not logits:
no sigmoid or per-image min/max normalization. Macdora limits the longest
working side to 1024 pixels without changing the saved/exported photo size.

Macdora first obtains automatic foreground/background seeds from BiRefNet Lite,
then estimates continuous alpha in the uncertain edge band with ViTMatte.
Seed quality limits the result: this does not guarantee recovery of missing
hair or transparency deep inside a region classified as opaque foreground.
Super detail is experimental and is not always better for every photograph.

ONNX Runtime Web 1.23.2 (MIT) uses WebGPU when selected/available, with WASM CPU
fallback in Auto mode. The first BiRefNet stage uses CPU. Some WebGPU graph
operators may also execute on CPU. No CUDA installation or paid API is needed.
Downloads are on-demand, immutable, SHA-256 checked and cached. Photos are
processed locally; no photo or alpha data is sent to inference servers.
